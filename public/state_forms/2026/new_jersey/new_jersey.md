### New Jersey has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form NJ-1040 - For Full Residents: The standard resident individual income tax return for New Jersey.

Form NJ-1040NR - For Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return for nonresidents and part-year residents with New Jersey income.

Form NJ-1040-V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher sent with paper payments for tax owed on filed returns.

Form NJ-1040X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended individual income tax return used to correct errors on prior filings.

Form NJ-1040-ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Vouchers for making estimated individual income tax payments.
