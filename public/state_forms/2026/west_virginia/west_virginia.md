### West Virginia has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form IT-140 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The standard individual income tax return used by all West Virginia taxpayers.

Schedule A - For Part/Non Residents: Attached to Form IT-140 to allocate income for nonresidents and part-year residents.

Form IT-140V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher submitted with checks or money orders for tax due.

Form IT-140X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended individual income tax return used to correct errors on prior filings.

Form IT-140ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Vouchers for making estimated individual income tax payments.
