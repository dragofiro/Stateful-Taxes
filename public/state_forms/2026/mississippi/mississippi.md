### Mississippi has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form 80-105 - For Full Residents: The resident individual income tax return for Mississippi.

Form 80-205 - For Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return for nonresidents and part-year residents with Mississippi income.

Form 80-115 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher to send when mailing a payment for individual income tax due.

Form 80-105/205 (Amended Box) - For Full/Part/Non Residents: Mississippi requires checking the amended box on the standard return forms to file an amendment.

Form 80-106 - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Payment vouchers for estimated individual income tax payments.
