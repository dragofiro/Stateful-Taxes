### North Carolina has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form D-400 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return form used by all North Carolina taxpayers.

Form D-400 Schedule PN - For Part/Non Residents: The schedule attached to Form D-400 to calculate North Carolina taxable income for nonresidents and part-year residents.

Form D-400V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher for mailing payments for tax due.

Form D-400 (Amended Box) - For Full/Part/Non Residents: North Carolina requires re-filing Form D-400 with the "Amended Return" bubble filled to correct past returns.

Form NC-40 - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Payment vouchers for making quarterly estimated individual income tax payments.
