### Oklahoma has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form 511 - For Full Residents: The standard resident individual income tax return for Oklahoma.

Form 511NR - For Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return for nonresidents and part-year residents with Oklahoma income.

Form 511-V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher for mailing check payments with paper tax returns.

Form 511-X - For Full Residents: The amended resident income tax return used to correct prior filings.

Form 511NR-X - For Part/Non Residents: The amended nonresident/part-year resident income tax return.

Form 514-DEP - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Vouchers for paying estimated individual income tax.
