### Maryland has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form 502 - For Full Residents: The standard resident individual income tax return for Maryland.

Form 505 - For Non Residents: The individual income tax return for nonresidents earning income in Maryland.

Form 502PY - For Part Residents: The individual income tax return specifically for part-year Maryland residents.

Form 502E - For Full/Part/Non Residents: Used to request an extension of time to file or make an extension payment.

Form 502V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher submitted with checks or money orders for unpaid tax balances.

Form 502X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended individual income tax return to correct previously filed returns.

Form 502D - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Declaration and vouchers for estimated individual income tax payments.
