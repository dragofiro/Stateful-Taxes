### North Dakota has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form ND-1 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return used by all North Dakota taxpayers.

Schedule ND-1NR - For Part/Non Residents: The schedule used with Form ND-1 to calculate tax for nonresidents and part-year residents.

Form ND-1V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher sent with mail-in payments for individual income tax.

Form ND-1X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended individual income tax return to correct mistakes on a prior return.

Form ND-1ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Payment vouchers for estimated individual income tax.
