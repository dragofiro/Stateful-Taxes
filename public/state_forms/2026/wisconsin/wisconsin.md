### Wisconsin has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form 1 - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for full-year Wisconsin residents.

Form 1NPR - For Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return for nonresidents and part-year residents with Wisconsin income.

Form PS - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher to submit when mailing payments for tax owed.

Form 1X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended individual income tax return to correct prior Wisconsin tax returns.

Form 1-ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Vouchers for submitting estimated individual income tax payments.
