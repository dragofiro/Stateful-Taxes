### Vermont has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form IN-111 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The primary individual income tax return for all Vermont taxpayers.

Schedule IN-113 - For Part/Non Residents: Attached to Form IN-111 to allocate income earned in and out of Vermont.

Form IN-116 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher included when paying individual income tax by mail.

Form IN-111 (Amended Box) - For Full/Part/Non Residents: Filed by re-submitting Form IN-111 with the "Amended" checkbox marked.

Form IN-114 - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Vouchers for making estimated individual income tax payments.
