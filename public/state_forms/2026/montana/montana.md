### Montana has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form Form IT - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The consolidated individual income tax return used by all Montana taxpayers.

Schedule NR - For Part/Non Residents: Used with Form IT to calculate income allocated to Montana for part-year and nonresidents.

Form Form IT-V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher included when mailing tax payments for balances due.

Form Form IT (Amended Box) - For Full/Part/Non Residents: Filed by re-submitting Form IT with the "Amended Return" checkbox marked.

Form Form ES-V - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Vouchers for making estimated individual income tax payments.
