### New York has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form IT-201 - For Full Residents: The standard resident individual income tax return for New York State.

Form IT-203 - For Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return for nonresidents and part-year residents of New York State.

Form IT-201-V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher included when mailing payments for income tax owed.

Form IT-201-X - For Full Residents: The amended resident income tax return to correct a previously filed IT-201.

Form IT-203-X - For Part/Non Residents: The amended nonresident/part-year resident income tax return to correct a previously filed IT-203.

Form IT-2105 - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Vouchers for submitting estimated individual income tax payments.
