### Nebraska has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form 1040N - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The standard individual income tax return used by all taxpayers in Nebraska.

Schedule III - For Part/Non Residents: Attached to Form 1040N to calculate tax for nonresidents and part-year residents.

Form 1040N-V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher for mailing checks or money orders for tax due.

Form 1040X-N - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended individual income tax return used to adjust prior Nebraska returns.

Form 1040N-ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Payment vouchers for quarterly estimated individual income tax payments.
