### Minnesota has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form M1 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The primary individual income tax return for all Minnesota taxpayers.

Schedule M1NR - For Part/Non Residents: Attached to Form M1 to determine income allocated to Minnesota for part-year residents and nonresidents.

Form M1W - For Full/Part/Non Residents: Schedule used to report Minnesota income tax withholding details.

Form M1X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended individual income tax return used to adjust previously filed Minnesota returns.

Form M14 - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Payment vouchers for estimated individual income tax.
