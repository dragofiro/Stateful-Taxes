### Arizona has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form 140 - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for full-year Arizona residents.

Form 140NR - For Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return for nonresidents who earned income from Arizona sources.

Form 140PY - For Part Residents: The individual income tax return specifically for part-year Arizona residents.

Form 140V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher to submit when mailing a payment for a balance due on your return.

Form 140X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended individual income tax return used to correct previously filed Arizona tax returns.

Form 140ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: The declaration and payment voucher for estimated individual income tax.
