### Colorado has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:
DR 0104 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The single individual income tax return form used by all Colorado filers regardless of residency status.

DR 0104PN - For Part/Non Residents to be filled out in addition to DR 0104: The part-year resident/nonresident tax calculation schedule used to apportion income to Colorado.

DR 0900 - For Full/Part/Non Residents Paying by Check or Money Order: The individual income tax payment voucher used when mailing a balance due.
