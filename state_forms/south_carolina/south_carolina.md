### South Carolina has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form SC1040 - For Full Residents: The individual income tax return for full-year South Carolina residents.

Form SC1040TC - For Part/Non Residents: Used alongside Form SC1040 and Schedule NR to calculate credits and non-resident income adjustments.

Schedule NR - For Part/Non Residents: Attached to Form SC1040 to apportion income for nonresidents and part-year residents.

Form SC1040-V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher for sending tax payments by mail.

Form SC1040-X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended individual income tax return for making corrections to prior filings.

Form SC1040ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Payment vouchers for estimated individual income tax.
