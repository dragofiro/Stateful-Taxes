### Iadho has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:
Form 40 - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for full-year Idaho residents.

Form 43 - For Part/Non Residents: The income tax return used by part-year residents and nonresidents who have income from Idaho sources.

Form 39R - For Full Residents to be filled out in addition to Form 40: The resident supplemental schedule used alongside Form 43 for certain adjustments or additions.

Form 39NR - For Part/Non Residents to be filled out in addition to Form 40: The part-year resident and nonresident supplemental schedule for Form 43.

Form 24 - For Full/Part Residents: The food tax credit refund form and instructions.

Form 51 - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: The estimated payment form for individual income tax.

Form ID-VP - For Paying by Check or Money Order when tax returns were filed electronically or mailed without a check: The income tax payment voucher used when mailing a balance due.
