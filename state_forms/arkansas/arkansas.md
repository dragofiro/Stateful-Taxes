### Arkansas has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form AR1000F - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for full-year Arkansas residents.

Form AR1000NR - For Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return for part-year residents and nonresidents with Arkansas income.

Form AR1000V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher used to mail in a tax payment with your return.

Form AR1000X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The form used to amend a previously filed individual income tax return in Arkansas.

Form AR1000ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Payment vouchers for making quarterly estimated individual income tax payments.
