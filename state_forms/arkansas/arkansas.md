### Arkansas has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:
AR1000F - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for full-year Arkansas residents.

AR1000NR - For Part/Non Residents: The income tax return used by part-year residents and nonresidents with Arkansas-source income.

AR1000V - For Full/Part/Non Residents Paying by Check or Money Order: The individual income tax payment voucher used when mailing a balance due.
