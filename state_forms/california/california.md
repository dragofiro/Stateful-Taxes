### California has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:
Form 540 - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for full-year California residents.

Form 540NR - For Part/Non Residents: The income tax return used by part-year residents and nonresidents with California-source income.

FTB 3582 - For Full/Part/Non Residents who e-file and pay by check or money order: The payment voucher for individual e-filed returns.
