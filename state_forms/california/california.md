### California has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form 540 - For Full Residents: The main individual income tax return for full-year California residents.

Form 540 2EZ - For Full Residents: A simplified California individual income tax return for qualifying residents with straightforward tax situations.

Form 540NR - For Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return for nonresidents and part-year residents with California-source income.

Form 540-V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher sent with paper checks or money orders for balances due.

Form 540X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: Used to amend and correct a previously filed California individual income tax return.

Form 540-ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Vouchers for making estimated income tax payments to the California Franchise Tax Board.

Form FTB 3519 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: Payment voucher used when making an extension payment for individual income tax.
