### Delaware has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:
Form 200-01 - For Full/Part Residents: The resident individual income tax return; part-year residents may file this or Form 200-02, whichever produces a lower tax.

Form 200-02 - For Part/Non Residents: The nonresident individual income tax return, apportioned by Delaware-source income.

Form 200V - For Full/Part/Non Residents Paying by Check or Money Order: The individual income tax payment voucher used when mailing a balance due.
