### Hawaii has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:
Form N-11 - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for full-year Hawaii residents.

Form N-15 - For Part/Non Residents: The income tax return used by part-year residents and nonresidents with Hawaii-source income.

Form N-200V - For Full/Part/Non Residents Paying by Check or Money Order: The individual income tax payment voucher used when mailing a balance due.
