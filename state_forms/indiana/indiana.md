### Indiana has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:
Form IT-40 For Full Residents: Standard individual tax income form for Indiana.

Form IT-40 PNR For Part/Non Residents: Tax income form for individuals not living in Indiana who earned income within Indiana.

Form IT-40 RNR For Reciprocal Non-Residents: Tax income form for residents of Reciprocal States (Kentucky, Michigan, Ohio, Pennsylvania, or Wisconsin) who made income in Indiana.

Form SC-40 For Full Resident Seniors: For residents aged 65 or older who meet specific income limits (<2500 for single seniors, <3500 for married seniors with a spouse under 65, <5000 for married seniors with both over 65)

Form IT-40 X For Full/Part/Non Residents: Used to correct any of the other tax forms in the event they were filled out incorrectly.
