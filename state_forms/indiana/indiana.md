### Indiana has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:
IT-40 - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for full-year Indiana residents.

IT-40PNR - For Part/Non Residents: The income tax return used by part-year residents and full-year nonresidents with Indiana-source income.

ES-40 - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: The estimated tax payment form for individual income tax.
