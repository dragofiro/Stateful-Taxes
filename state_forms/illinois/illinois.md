### Illinois has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:
Form IL-505-I For Full/Part/Non Residents: Use this for making an Automatic Extension Payment if you need more time to file your tax return (You must still make your payment on time).

Form IL-1040 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The primary Individual Income Tax Return form you'll file as a resident, part-year resident, or nonresident.

Form IL-1040-ES For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Used for sending in Estimated Income Tax Payments for the tax year if you don't have enough withheld.

Form IL-1040-V For Full/Part/Non Residents to be mailed with your 1040 form: A Payment Voucher to mail in with a balance due on your IL-1040 return (This form is a check to make a payment for your 1040 form).

Form IL-1040-X For Full/Part/Non Residents: Use this if you need to file an Amended Individual Income Tax Return to fix a mistake on a previous year's return.

Form IL-1040-X-V For Full/Part/Non Residents to be mailed with your 1040-X form: A Payment Voucher to mail in with a balance due on your IL-1040-X return (This form is a check to make a payment for your 1040-X form).

Schedule CR: Used to claim a Credit for Tax Paid to Other States.

Schedule IL-E/EITC: Used to calculate your Illinois Exemption and Earned Income Tax Credit.

Schedule M: Used to claim Other Additions and Subtractions for individuals (like medical debt relief or retirement subtractions).

Schedule NR: Required for Nonresident and Part-Year Resident Computation of your Illinois tax.
