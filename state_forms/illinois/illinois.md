### Illinois has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:
IL-1040 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return used by all Illinois filers.

Schedule NR - For Part/Non Residents to be filled out in addition to IL-1040: Computes the Illinois tax owed by part-year residents and nonresidents based on Illinois-source income.

IL-1040-V - For Full/Part/Non Residents Paying by Check or Money Order: The payment voucher used when mailing a balance due.
