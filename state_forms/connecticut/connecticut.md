### Connecticut has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form CT-1040 - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for full-year Connecticut residents.

Form CT-1040NR/PY - For Part/Non Residents: The income tax return for part-year residents and nonresidents with Connecticut income.

Form CT-1040V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher for mailing a check or money order for individual income tax owed.

Form CT-1040X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The form used to amend a previously filed Connecticut individual income tax return.

Form CT-1040ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Payment vouchers for making quarterly estimated individual income tax payments.
