### Connecticut has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:
CT-1040 - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for full-year Connecticut residents.

CT-1040NR/PY - For Part/Non Residents: The income tax return used by part-year residents and nonresidents with Connecticut-source income.

CT-1040V - For Full/Part/Non Residents Paying by Check or Money Order: The electronic filing payment voucher used when mailing a balance due.
