### Iowa has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:
Form IA 1040 For Full/Part/Non Residents: The standard individual tax income form for Iowa.

Form IA 1040V For Full/Part/Non Residents: The standard payment voucher form for Iowa to be sent in with Form IA 1040.

Form IA 1040X For Full/Part/Non Residents: The form for amending any mistakes made on 1040 forms.

Form IA 1040 Schedule B For Full/Part/Non Residents: Only filled out when interest or dividend income passes $1500 per year.

Form IA 2210 For Full/Part/Non Residents: For when not enough was witheld from your paychecks.

Form IA 8453 For Full/Part/Non Residents: For verifying electronic tax returns (if you use professional preparers or an electronic return this will be necessary).

Schedule IA 126 For Part/Non Residents: For claiming credit so non residents only pay taxes for income earned in Iowa.
