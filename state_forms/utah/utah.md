### Utah has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form TC-40 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return form used by all Utah taxpayers.

Form TC-40B - For Part/Non Residents: The schedule attached to Form TC-40 for nonresidents and part-year residents to calculate Utah income.

Form TC-546 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher used when mailing a check for income tax owed.

Form TC-40 (Amended Box) - For Full/Part/Non Residents: Utah requires submitting a revised Form TC-40 with the "Amended" box marked to make corrections.

Form TC-546E - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Payment vouchers for estimated individual income tax.
