### Georgia has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:
Form 500 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return used by all Georgia filers; part-year residents and nonresidents complete the attached Schedule 3 to apportion income.

Form 525-TV - For Full/Part/Non Residents Paying by Check or Money Order: The individual and fiduciary payment voucher used when mailing a balance due.
