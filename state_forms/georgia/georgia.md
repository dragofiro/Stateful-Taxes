### Georgia has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form 500 - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for full-year Georgia residents.

Form 500NPR - For Part/Non Residents: The schedule used alongside Form 500 by nonresidents and part-year residents to calculate Georgia-taxable income.

Form 520V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher to mail in with your tax payment when filing a return.

Form 500-X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The form used to file an amended individual income tax return in Georgia.

Form 500ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Vouchers for submitting estimated individual income tax payments throughout the year.
