### The District of Columbia has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:
D-40 - For Full/Part Residents: The individual income tax return for DC residents (including part-year residents). DC does not tax the wage income of nonresidents who work in DC but live elsewhere, so there is no separate DC nonresident return for wage earners.

D-40V - For Full/Part Residents Paying by Check or Money Order: The individual income tax payment voucher used when mailing a balance due.
