### Virginia has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form 760 - For Full Residents: The standard resident individual income tax return for Virginia.

Form 763 - For Non Residents: The individual income tax return for nonresidents who earned income in Virginia.

Form 760PY - For Part Residents: The individual income tax return specifically for part-year Virginia residents.

Form 760-PMT - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher sent with mailed paper checks for tax owed.

Form 760X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended individual income tax return used to correct previously filed returns.

Form 760ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Vouchers for submitting quarterly estimated income tax payments.
