### Delaware has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form PIT-RES - For Full Residents: The standard personal income tax return for full-year Delaware residents.

Form PIT-NON - For Part/Non Residents: The personal income tax return for part-year residents and nonresidents earning income in Delaware.

Form PIT-V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher to submit when mailing a tax payment with your Delaware return.

Form PIT-AMN - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended Delaware individual income tax return to fix mistakes on prior returns.

Form PIT-EST - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Payment vouchers for quarterly estimated individual income tax.
