### Alabama has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form 40 - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for full-year Alabama residents.

Form 40NR - For Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return for nonresidents and part-year residents with Alabama income.

Form 40V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax payment voucher to mail in with any balance due.

Form 40X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended individual income tax return used to correct mistakes on a previously filed return.

Form 40ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Used to make quarterly estimated individual income tax payments.
