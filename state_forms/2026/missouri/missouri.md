### Missouri has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form MO-1040 - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for full-year Missouri residents.

Form MO-1040P - For Full Residents: A simplified individual income tax return for residents with uncomplicated tax profiles.

Form MO-NRI - For Part/Non Residents: The schedule attached to Form MO-1040 to calculate non-resident income proportions.

Form MO-1040V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: Payment voucher used when submitting paper check payments for tax owed.

Form MO-1040X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended individual income tax return used to correct past Missouri filings.

Form MO-1040ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Vouchers for submitting quarterly estimated income tax payments.
