### Louisiana has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form IT-540 - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for full-year Louisiana residents.

Form IT-540B - For Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return for nonresidents and part-year residents with Louisiana sources of income.

Form IT-540-V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher to submit when paying individual income tax by mail.

Form IT-540-X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The form used to file an amended Louisiana individual income tax return.

Form IT-540ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Vouchers used to make estimated individual income tax payments.
