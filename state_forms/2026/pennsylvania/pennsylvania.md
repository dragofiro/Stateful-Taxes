### Pennsylvania has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form PA-40 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The primary personal income tax return used by all taxpayers earning income in Pennsylvania.

Schedule NRH - For Non Residents: Used by nonresidents to apportion income earned partly inside and outside Pennsylvania.

Form PA-40 V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher submitted with paper tax payments by mail.

Form PA-40X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended personal income tax return used to make corrections to prior filings.

Form PA-40ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Payment vouchers for quarterly estimated tax payments.
