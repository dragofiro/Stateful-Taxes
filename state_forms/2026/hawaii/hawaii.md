### Hawaii has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form N-11 - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for residents filing federal income tax returns.

Form N-15 - For Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return for nonresidents and part-year residents earning Hawaii income.

Form N-200V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher for sending a tax payment by mail for an individual return.

Form N-11/N-15 (Amended Box) - For Full/Part/Non Residents: Hawaii uses its standard returns with the "Amended" checkbox checked to file corrected returns.

Form N-200PX - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Payment vouchers for making estimated individual income tax payments.
