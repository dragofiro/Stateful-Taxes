### Puerto Rico has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form 482 (Formulario 482) - For Full Residents: The primary individual income tax return used by resident individuals in Puerto Rico.   
DocHub

Form 482.0 (Formulario 482.0) - For Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return used by nonresidents or individuals who moved into or out of Puerto Rico during the year.

Form 480.30 - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Used for filing and submitting estimated individual income tax payments throughout the year.

Form SC 2644 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The form used to request an automatic extension of time to file the Puerto Rico individual tax return.   
DocHub

Form 483.20 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The exception form required if you are submitting a paper return instead of filing electronically.
