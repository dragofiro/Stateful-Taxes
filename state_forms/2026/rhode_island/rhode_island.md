### Rhode Island has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form RI-1040 - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for full-year Rhode Island residents.

Form RI-1040NR - For Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return for nonresidents and part-year residents with Rhode Island income.

Form RI-1040V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher sent when submitting checks for tax due.

Form RI-1040X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended individual income tax return to correct previously filed tax returns.

Form RI-1040ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Vouchers for making quarterly estimated income tax payments.
