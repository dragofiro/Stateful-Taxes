### Michigan has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form MI-1040 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The standard individual income tax return for all Michigan taxpayers.

Schedule NR - For Part/Non Residents: Attached to Form MI-1040 to allocate income for nonresidents and part-year residents.

Form MI-1040-V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher sent when mailing checks or money orders for tax owed.

Form MI-1040X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended individual income tax return used to correct errors on prior Michigan filings.

Form MI-1040ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Vouchers for making estimated individual income tax payments.

Form MI-1040CR - For Full Residents: Form used to claim the Michigan Homestead Property Tax Credit.
