### Colorado has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form DR 0104 - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for full-year Colorado residents.

Form DR 0104PN - For Part/Non Residents: The tax schedule required alongside Form DR 0104 to calculate tax for part-year residents and nonresidents.

Form DR 0104EP - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher for making estimated individual income tax payments.

Form DR 0104V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher included when mailing a payment with your paper return.

Form DR 0104X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended individual income tax return used to adjust previously reported income or credits.
