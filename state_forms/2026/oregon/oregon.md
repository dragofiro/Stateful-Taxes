### Oregon has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form OR-40 - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for full-year Oregon residents.

Form OR-40-N - For Non Residents: The individual income tax return for nonresidents earning Oregon-source income.

Form OR-40-P - For Part Residents: The individual income tax return for part-year residents of Oregon.

Form OR-40-V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher included when mailing tax payments by check or money order.

Form OR-40-X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended individual income tax return used to fix errors on past filings.

Form OR-40-V (Estimated) - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Vouchers for making estimated income tax payments.
