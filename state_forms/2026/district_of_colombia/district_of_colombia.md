### The district of Colombia has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:
Form D-40 - For Full/Part Residents: The standard individual income tax return for Washington, D.C. residents and part-year residents.   

Form D-40B - For Non Residents: The non-resident request for refund of D.C. income tax withheld in error.

Form D-40ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Declaration and payment vouchers for estimated individual income tax.

Form D-40V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: Payment voucher sent when mailing checks or money orders for a balance due.

Form D-2000 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: Extension of time to file form used to request additional time or make an extension payment.
