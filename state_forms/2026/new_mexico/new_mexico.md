### New Mexico has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form PIT-1 - For Full Residents: The standard personal income tax return for full-year New Mexico residents.

Form PIT-B - For Part/Non Residents: The schedule attached to Form PIT-1 for allocation and apportionment of income for part-year and nonresidents.

Form PIT-PV - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher used when paying tax due by check or money order.

Form PIT-X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended personal income tax return used to fix errors on a previously filed return.

Form PIT-ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Payment vouchers for estimated personal income tax.
