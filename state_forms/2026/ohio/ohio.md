### Ohio has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form IT 1040 - For Full Residents: The main individual income tax return for full-year Ohio residents.

Form IT 1040 (Schedule of Income) - For Part/Non Residents: Includes IT NRC/PY schedules to adjust income for nonresidents and part-year residents.

Form IT 1040-V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher sent with checks or money orders for tax payments owed.

Form IT 1040X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended individual income tax return used to correct prior Ohio tax returns.

Form IT 1040ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Vouchers for making estimated individual income tax payments.
