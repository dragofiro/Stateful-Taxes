### Kansas has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:
Form K-40 For Full/Part/Non Residents: The standard Kansas individual tax return. (amendments to taxes paid are also made through this form)

Form K-40V For Full/Part/Non Residents: Payment voucher to be sent along with the K-40 form.

Form k-210 For Full/Part/Non Residents: For calculating underpayment of estimated individual taxes due.

Schedule A For Full/Part/Non Residents: For listing itemized deductions from your individual taxes.

Schedule S For Full/Part/Non Residents: For modifying the main K-40 form to represent accurate numbers on taxable income.
