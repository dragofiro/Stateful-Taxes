### Kentucky has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form 740 - For Full Residents: The main individual income tax return for full-year Kentucky residents.

Form 740-NP - For Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return for nonresidents and part-year residents with Kentucky income.

Form 740-NP-R - For Reciprocal Non-Residents: The individual tax return for residents of reciprocal states (IL, IN, MI, OH, VA, WV, WI) who worked in Kentucky.

Form 740-V - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher to mail in with payments owed on individual tax returns.

Form 740-X - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The amended individual income tax return used to correct prior filings in Kentucky.

Form 740-ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Vouchers for making estimated individual income tax payments.
