### Arizona has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:
Form 140 - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for full-year Arizona residents.

Form 140PY - For Part Residents: The income tax return for taxpayers who moved into or out of Arizona during the year.

Form 140NR - For Non Residents: The income tax return for nonresidents with Arizona-source income.

Form 140V - For Full/Part/Non Residents Paying by Check or Money Order: The individual income tax payment voucher used when mailing a balance due.
