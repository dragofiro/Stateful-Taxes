### Maine has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form 1040ME - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The main individual income tax return used by all taxpayers in Maine.

Form 1040ME-PV - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher sent with checks or money orders for balances due.

Form 1040ME (Amended Box) - For Full/Part/Non Residents: Maine uses Form 1040ME marked as an amended return to correct prior returns.

Form 1040ES-ME - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Vouchers for sending quarterly estimated individual income tax payments.

Schedule NR - For Part/Non Residents: Required along with Form 1040ME to apportion income for nonresidents and part-year residents.
