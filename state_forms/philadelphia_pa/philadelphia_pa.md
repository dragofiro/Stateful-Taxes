Philadelphia, PA, has personal income taxes.

(Note: Philadelphia taxes personal earned income via local wage and net profits taxes rather than a traditional state-style 1040 return).

Important Forms:
Form Earnings Tax - For Non Residents / Partial Residents: Filed by non-residents working in Philadelphia whose employers did not withhold the local Philadelphia Wage Tax.

Form NPT - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: The Net Profits Tax return required for individuals, sole proprietors, and freelancers earning business net income in Philadelphia.   

Form SIT - For Full Residents: The School Income Tax return filed by Philadelphia residents receiving unearned income (such as dividends, interest, or royalties).

Form BIRT - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: The Business Income and Receipts Tax return filed by self-employed individuals doing business in Philadelphia.

Form NPT-EZ - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: A simplified Net Profits Tax form for qualifying small business owners and freelancers.
