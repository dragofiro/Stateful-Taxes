### Alabama has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:
Form 40 - For Full Residents: The standard individual income tax return for full-year Alabama residents.

Form 40A - For Full Residents (short form): A simplified return for full-year residents with interest/dividend income of $1,500 or less and no itemized deductions.

Form 40NR - For Part/Non Residents: The income tax return used by part-year residents and nonresidents with Alabama-source income.

Form 40V - For Full/Part/Non Residents Paying by Check or Money Order: The income tax payment voucher used when mailing a balance due for Form 40, 40A, or 40NR.

NR-AF2 - For Nonresident Sellers of Alabama Real Property: Affidavit of Seller's Gain, used to calculate withholding on the sale of Alabama real estate by a nonresident.
