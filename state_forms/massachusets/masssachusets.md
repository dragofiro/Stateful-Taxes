### Massachusets has personal income taxes.

## Important Forms:

Form 1 - For Full Residents: The standard resident individual income tax return for Massachusetts.

Form 1-NR/PY - For Part/Non Residents: The individual income tax return for nonresidents and part-year residents with Massachusetts income.

Form PV - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The payment voucher included when mailing payments for balances due on individual returns.

Form CA-6 - For Full/Part/Non Residents: The application for abatement or amended return used to correct mistakes on filed returns.

Form Form 1-ES - For Freelancers/Self-Employed Full/Part/Non Residents: Vouchers for paying estimated individual income taxes.
